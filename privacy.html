Nova Detailing – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 7, 2026

Business Name: Nova Detailing

Website: ajdetailing.store

1. What This Policy Covers

This policy explains how Nova Detailing collects and uses information when you browse this website, request detailing service, or use Nova or Nova Voice.

2. Information We Collect

  • Booking information: your name, phone number, optional email, service address, requested date, package, vehicle details, and notes you choose to provide.
  • Service records: appointment, quote, payment, and service information needed to operate the business.
  • Vehicle media: before-and-after photos or videos when taken for service records or when you agree to marketing use.
  • Security data: limited technical information used for fraud, spam, rate-limit, and abuse prevention.

3. How We Use Information

  • Review, quote, schedule, and provide detailing services.
  • Contact you about a booking request or appointment.
  • Maintain business and service records.
  • Protect the website and AI assistants from spam or misuse.
  • Improve the customer experience and service operations.

4. Nova and Nova Voice

Nova is the text chatbot. Text you send to Nova is transmitted to xAI so the assistant can generate a reply.

Nova Voice is the speech-only AI assistant. Microphone capture begins only after you choose Start speaking and grant microphone permission. Audio you intentionally send is transmitted to xAI so Nova Voice can process your question and generate a spoken response. If you use the Nova Voice phone option, the call is handled by the voice service connected to that number.

Nova and Nova Voice are separate from the booking form. Do not send payment credentials or other highly sensitive information to either AI assistant. Third-party AI providers may process or retain data under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Website and Service Providers

Nova Detailing uses service providers to operate parts of the website. Supabase processes booking requests and supports AI session and abuse-prevention infrastructure. Cloudflare Turnstile may process security-verification data before an AI session begins. xAI processes the text or audio you intentionally send to the AI assistants.

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a legitimate transfer of the business.

6. Payments and Deposits

Payment or deposit instructions may be provided when your appointment is confirmed. The website booking form does not ask for full card or bank-account credentials, and Nova Detailing does not intentionally store those credentials through this form.

7. Photos and Marketing

Vehicle photos may be taken as part of documenting the service. Use of identifiable vehicle photos for public marketing, social media, or the website is based on customer permission. Marketing permission is optional and may be withdrawn for future use by contacting Nova Detailing.

8. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information, but no online system can guarantee absolute security. Information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for scheduling, service, customer support, security, accounting, legal, or business-record purposes.

9. Your Choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete information Nova Detailing maintains about you, subject to legal and legitimate business-record requirements. You may also decline or withdraw future marketing use of your vehicle photos. Use the booking/contact process on the website to make a request.

10. Children

The website and detailing services are not directed to children under 13, and Nova Detailing does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the booking process.

11. Updates

This policy may be updated as the website or services change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current version.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or data practices can be submitted through the booking/contact process at ajdetailing.store.

Your Acknowledgment

By using the website, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.
