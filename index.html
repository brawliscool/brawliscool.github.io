05 / BEFORE YOU BOOK

THE GOOD
TO KNOW.

Still have a question? Ask Nova anytime using the AI button.

Do you come to my home or workplace?

Yes. We're mobile. Send your service address and we'll confirm the location, access and any water or power requirements beforehand.

How do you determine the final price?

Vehicle size, condition, and any extra cleaning required. Package prices are starting points; your quote is confirmed before work begins.

Which package should I choose?

Refresh is ideal for regular upkeep, Full Detail adds deeper interior care, and Complete adds extra interior and paint-protection attention.

Are you servicing Longview?

Ask about current Longview and surrounding-area availability when you submit your request. We confirm service areas individually.

Does a request confirm my appointment?

Not yet. We'll contact you personally to confirm pricing, location and your appointment time before it is booked.
