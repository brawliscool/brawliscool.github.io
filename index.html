04 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few details
before your detail.

Do you come to my home or workplace?

Yes. Nova is a mobile service. Share your location and we’ll confirm access, workspace and any water or power needs before your appointment.

What affects my final price?

Vehicle size, condition and any extra cleaning needed. The listed prices are starting prices. I’ll confirm your quote before work begins.

Which package should I choose?

Refresh suits routine upkeep. Full Detail adds deeper interior care. Complete adds more intensive cleaning and paint protection. Send photos if you’re unsure.

Are you detailing in Longview yet?

The move to Longview is planned for November 2026. Contact me to ask about upcoming availability; current appointments are based in Fairfield.

Is my appointment confirmed when I request it?

No. Requests are followed up personally to confirm the price, location and available time. Your appointment is booked once we agree on those details.
